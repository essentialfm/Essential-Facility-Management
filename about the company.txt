About Us

Essential Facility Management (EFM) is a Lagos-based property and estate management company committed to delivering exceptional, reliable facility solutions across Nigeria.

Since our founding in March 2023, we’ve grown from the ground up, built not on big marketing budgets, but on trust, referrals, and consistent, quality service. 

Today, we manage properties across Lagos and Abuja, offering end-to-end solutions spanning property management, leasing, tenant placement, maintenance coordination, and day-to-day operations.

What sets us apart is our hands-on approach. We don’t just manage properties - we protect and grow their value, while ensuring tenants and property owners alike experience seamless, professional service at every touchpoint. Every property we manage reflects our core belief: no job is complete until the client is completely satisfied.

As we continue to expand, our commitment remains the same, combining professionalism, innovation, and genuine care to create exceptional living and working environments for the people who trust us with their properties.
